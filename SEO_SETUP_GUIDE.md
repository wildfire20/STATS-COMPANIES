## Step 2: Set Up Google Search Console (FREE)

Google Search Console tells Google your website exists and monitors how it appears in search results.

### How to Set Up:

1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click **"Add Property"**
4. Choose **"URL prefix"** and enter: `https://statscompanies.co.za`
5. Choose **HTML tag verification**:
   - Google will give you a meta tag like: `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="abc123..." />`
   - Send me this code and I'll add it to your website
6. Click **Verify**

### After Verification:
1. Go to **"Sitemaps"** in the left menu
2. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Submit**

This tells Google about all your pages!

## Step 4: Set Up Google Business Profile (CRITICAL for Local SEO)

This is the MOST important step for appearing in Google searches like "printing near me" or "photography Pretoria".

### How to Set Up:

1. Go to [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click **"Add your business to Google"**
4. Enter your business name: `STATS Companies`
5. Choose category: `Printing Service` (you can add more later)
6. Add your physical address (if you have one)
7. Add your phone number
8. Add your website: `https://statscompanies.co.za`
9. Verify your business (Google will send a postcard or call)

### After Verification:
- Add business hours
- Add photos of your work
- Add all your services:
  - Digital Printing
  - Photography
  - Videography
  - Digital Marketing
- Respond to reviews
- Post updates regularly
